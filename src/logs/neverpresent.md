---
title: "Neverpresent"
date: "2025-11-30"
author: "Van Carina"
readtime: 4
---

You keep remembering the nightmares you’ve had.

The one you had yesterday, last week, last month, last year, since days gone by.

The wrong choices you made, the wrong words you said, the wrong things you did.

You kept apologizing to everyone, but it didn't occured to you that the one you haven't apologized to is yourself.

You keep having lofty dreams.

Imagining yourself tomorrow, next week, next month, next year, to eternity.

The grand design and ambition you’ve always longed for.

Your mind is always living in the past and in the future, but never in the now.

As your body hurtles away in the motions of reality.

Never content, never satisfied.

It needs you. To be here, to act at your command.

To deliver on your dreams and to come to terms with your past.

You are not that person from that day, nor the one that might be.

So find it in you to forgive yourself, and come stay in the present for a little while longer.
